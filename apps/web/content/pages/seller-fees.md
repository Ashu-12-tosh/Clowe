# Seller Fees

*Last updated: 21 October 2026*

What it costs to sell on Clowe. Registering and listing products are free. The amounts below are read from our current settings, so this page always shows the fees in force; your Payouts page and the pricing calculator show the exact amount on every order. Every Clowe charge is before GST: GST at {{cloweGst}} is added, and all of it appears on Clowe's monthly tax invoice to you, which you can download from Billing & Wallet.

## Clowe's fees on each unit sold

Percentages are of the selling price, which includes GST.

- **Referral fee:** {{referralFee}}. The product form shows the fee for your category at every price you try.
- **GT (goods transfer) charge:** {{gtFee}}.
- **Payment collection fee:** {{collectionFee}}, for online payment or Cash on Delivery collection.
- **Handling fee:** per unit, by the unit's selling price — {{handlingFees}}. No handling fee on the Premium plan.

## Courier charges

Delivery is free for buyers. You pay our courier partner's (Delhivery's) actual charges, each shown as its own line:

- **Forward freight** — for delivering each parcel. You see an estimate before you book.
- **COD fee** — on a Cash on Delivery parcel, the courier's fee for collecting the cash.
- **RTO freight** — if a parcel cannot be delivered and comes back to you.
- **Return pickup freight** — when you approve a return and the courier brings it back to you.

How each is worked out, with examples: [Delivery Charges Explained](/pages/delivery-charges).

## Seller plans

Plans renew every month on the same date and are paid from your Seller Wallet first, then from your next payout: {{sellerPlans}}. If a renewal cannot be paid you have {{planGraceDays}}, then the shop moves to Free; products over the Free limit are hidden, never deleted, and you choose which stay live.

## Advertising, sponsorship and AI Try-On

- **Advertising and sponsorship** are optional and priced on the rate cards in your dashboard (Advertising and Sponsorship pages). New sellers get {{welcomeAdCredit}} of advertising credit when approved. Shoppers always see a "Sponsored" or "Ad" label.
- **AI Try-On credits:** one credit for each successful try-on image; failed ones, and the same shopper trying the same photo on the same product again within 24 hours, are free. Packs: {{tryonPacks}} (before GST), once or every month; above the largest pack, {{tryonExtraCredit}} a credit through vendor support. {{tryonFreeGrant}}. Unused {{tryonExpiry}}.

## Penalties

- **Late dispatch:** {{penaltyAmount}}, once per order, if it is not dispatched within {{penaltyAfter}} of being placed. (Please dispatch within {{dispatchSla}}.)
- **Counterfeit or non-compliant listing:** {{counterfeitPenalty}} per instance.
- **Inaccurate product data supplied for AI Try-On:** {{tryonDataPenalty}} per instance.

Each penalty is applied with its reason, shown on your Penalties page, and deducted from your next payout, plus GST at {{cloweGst}}, which appears on Clowe's monthly tax invoice. A waived penalty's GST is returned with it.

We announce any change at least 30 days before it takes effect, on your dashboard, by SMS and by email. Orders placed before the change keep the fees they were placed under.
