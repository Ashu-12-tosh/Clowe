# Seller Fees

*Last updated: 16 October 2026*

What it costs to sell on Clowe. Registering and listing products are free. The amounts below are read from our current settings, so this page always shows the fees in force; your Payouts page and the pricing calculator show the exact amount on every order.

## Clowe's fees

- **Commission:** {{commission}} of your price before GST, for the units sold.
- **Payment gateway / collection fee:** {{gatewayFee}} of what the buyer pays, for online payment or Cash on Delivery collection.
- **Platform fee:** {{platformFee}} per order line.
- **Closing fee:** {{closingFee}} per unit.
- **GT (goods transfer) charge:** {{gtCharge}} per unit.

## Courier charges

Delivery is free for buyers. You pay our courier partner's (Delhivery's) actual charges, each shown as its own line:

- **Forward freight** — for delivering each parcel. You see an estimate before you book.
- **COD fee** — on a Cash on Delivery parcel, the courier's fee for collecting the cash.
- **RTO freight** — if a parcel cannot be delivered and comes back to you.
- **Return pickup freight** — when you approve a return and the courier brings it back to you.
