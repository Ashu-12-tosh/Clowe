# Clowe App

## 📱 Coming soon

The Clowe app for Android and iOS is under development — with camera-first AI Try-On, push notifications for order updates, and one-tap PIN login.

## Until then

Clowe works beautifully in your phone's browser:

- Open **clowe** in Chrome or Safari on your phone
- Use *Add to Home Screen* for an app-like experience
- Everything works: try-on, orders, returns, seller dashboard

Want to be notified when the app launches? Keep notifications enabled in your profile preferences.
