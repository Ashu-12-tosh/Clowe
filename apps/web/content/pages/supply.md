# Supply to Clowe

Manufacture or wholesale clothing? Partner with us.

## Supply models

- **Marketplace seller** — list under your own shop name, set your own prices ([register here](/sell))
- **B2B supply** — bulk-supply to Clowe-affiliated sellers and private labels
- **Fabric & trims** — supply raw materials to our manufacturing partners

## What we look for

Consistent quality, honest lead times, and compliance with labour and safety standards. GST registration is required for B2B supply.

## Get in touch

Write to **supply@clowe.example** with your catalogue, capacity and location. Our sourcing team reviews every proposal.
