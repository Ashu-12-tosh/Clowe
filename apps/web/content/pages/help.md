# Help Centre

Quick answers to common questions. For anything else, the **Support chat** (gold bubble, bottom-right) is available on every page.

## Orders & delivery

- **Where is my order?** — My Orders → open the order → live status and courier tracking
- **Cancel an order** — possible until any item ships, from the order page
- **Delivery time** — typically 3–7 days depending on your pincode

## Returns & refunds

- **Return window** — request a return within 7 days of delivery from the order page
- **Refund timeline** — 5–7 business days after the item is received back

## Account

- **Login** — phone + OTP, or your 4-digit Clowe PIN
- **Change phone number** — Profile → Personal information → Change (OTP-verified)
- **Try-on photo** — Profile → Try-on photo (upload, replace or delete anytime)

## Sellers

- **Become a seller** — [Sell on Clowe](/sell)
- **Seller support** — from your seller dashboard, or the Support chat
