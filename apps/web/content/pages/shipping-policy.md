# Shipping Policy

- Orders above **₹499** ship free; below that a small delivery fee applies at checkout.
- Sellers hand over shipments to tracked courier partners; tracking is available in **My Orders** and [Track Order](/track).
- Standard delivery is **3–5 business days**; remote pincodes and bulky items can take longer.
- If a shipment is lost in transit, Clowe issues a full refund.
