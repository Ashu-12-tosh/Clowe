# Shipping Policy

*Last updated: 16 October 2026*

This policy covers orders placed on cloweshop.com, run by **{{legal.name}}**. Items are shipped by the sellers on Clowe through our courier partner, Delhivery.

## Delivery charge

- **Delivery is free on every order.** There is no minimum order value and no delivery fee at checkout.

## When your order ships and arrives

- Sellers must hand your order to the courier within {{dispatchSla}} of it being placed.
- Checkout and your order page show an estimated delivery date — usually **4–6 business days** for standard delivery. Remote pincodes and large items can take longer.
- When your item ships you get a tracking number (AWB). Follow it in **My Orders** or on [Track Order](/track).

## If something goes wrong

- **Lost in transit:** if the courier confirms your parcel is lost, our team refunds you in full.
- **Could not be delivered:** if a parcel cannot be delivered and goes back to the seller, a prepaid order is refunded to the original payment method.
- For anything else, use **My Orders** or [Contact Us](/contact).
