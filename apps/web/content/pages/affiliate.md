# Affiliate Program

Earn by recommending products you love.

## How it works

1. Apply with your channel (blog, social, video).
2. Get tracked links for any Clowe product or category.
3. Earn a commission on every qualifying purchase.

## Highlights

- Commissions across **all categories**
- 30-day tracking window
- Monthly payouts to your bank account
