# Return Policy

*Last updated: 16 October 2026*

This policy applies to orders placed on cloweshop.com, run by **{{legal.name}}**.

## The return window

- Most items can be returned within **{{returnWindow}}** of delivery.
- Some categories have a shorter window — grocery, for example. Each product page shows the window for that item, and your order keeps the window it was bought with.

## Items that cannot be returned

Products in these categories cannot be returned: **{{nonReturnableCategories}}**. Their product page, the cart and checkout all say "Not returnable", and their orders have no return button.

## How to return

1. Go to **My Orders**, open the order and choose **Return** on the item.
2. Pick a reason. If the item arrived damaged or is the wrong item, add photos — they help the seller decide.
3. The seller reviews your request. Clowe's support team can step in if you disagree with the decision.
4. Once approved, our courier partner picks the item up from your address **at no cost to you** and takes it back to the seller.

## Condition

Send the item back unused, with its original tags and packaging.

## Your refund

- The refund is made once the item reaches the seller and is checked.
- **Paid online:** it goes back to your original payment method. Banks usually show it within **5–7 business days**.
- **Cash on Delivery:** our support team will contact you to arrange your refund.
- If the item comes back damaged or not as sent, the refund is held while our team reviews it, using the seller's packing video of your order.
