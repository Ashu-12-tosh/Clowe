# Protect and Build Your Brand

Clowe takes counterfeits and brand misuse seriously.

## For brand owners

- **Verified brand tags** — prove ownership once, get a badge on every listing
- **Takedown requests** — report infringing listings; we act within 48 hours
- **Exclusive storefronts** — control how your brand appears on Clowe

## For sellers

Only list products you are authorised to sell. Listings with brand names are subject to verification, and repeated violations lead to suspension.

## Report a violation

Email **brand@clowe.example** with the listing link and proof of ownership. Our trust & safety team will investigate and respond.
