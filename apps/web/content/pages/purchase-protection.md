# Purchase Protection

*Last updated: 16 October 2026*

What Clowe does when an order goes wrong.

## What's covered

- **Item never arrives** — if the courier confirms it is lost, or it cannot be delivered and goes back to the seller, you get a full refund.
- **Damaged or wrong item** — return it within the return window with photos. Pickup is free; the refund is made once the item reaches the seller and is checked.
- **Safe payment** — payments go through PCI-DSS compliant gateways. Sellers are paid only after your order is delivered and a {{payoutHold}} holding period has passed, so money for a returned item is never already gone.
- **Every order is filmed** — sellers record a packing video of every order before it ships. It helps settle a dispute about what was in the box.

## How refunds work

Refunds go back to your original payment method; banks usually show them within **5–7 business days**. For Cash on Delivery orders, our support team will contact you to arrange the refund. You can follow every step in **My Orders**.

## Need help?

Use the Support chat (bottom-right) or raise a complaint from your order — every complaint gets a tracking ID. If it is not resolved, see [Grievance Redressal](/pages/grievance-redressal).
