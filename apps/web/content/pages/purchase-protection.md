# 100% Purchase Protection

Shop on Clowe with complete confidence.

## What's covered

- **Item not delivered** — full refund if your order never arrives
- **Damaged or defective** — return within the return window with photos; refund after pickup
- **Wrong item received** — we arrange pickup and a full refund or replacement
- **Payment protection** — all payments are processed through secure, PCI-compliant gateways; sellers are paid only after dispatch

## How refunds work

Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method within **5–7 business days**. You can track every step from **My Orders → Return status**.

## Need help?

Use the Support chat (bottom-right) for instant answers, or raise a complaint — every complaint gets a tracking ID and our team's attention.
