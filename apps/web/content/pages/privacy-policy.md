## 3. The AI Virtual Try-On Feature

3.1 What is collected. Where You use the AI Virtual Try-On, We collect the photograph, video or scan uploaded by You (the User Image), any avatar selection, and derived body-shape or measurement estimates generated from that image, together with session metadata.

3.2 Voluntary. Use of the feature is entirely voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing any Product.

3.3 Consent. By uploading a User Image You give Your express, specific and informed consent, within the meaning of Section 6 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Rule 5 of the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, to the collection, storage and automated processing of that image, including facial and body imagery, for the sole purpose of generating and displaying the try-on rendering to You.

3.4 Eligibility. The feature may be used only by persons aged eighteen (18) years or above. You must not upload an image of any person below that age, or of any other person without that person’s express and demonstrable consent.

3.5 Limits on use. We shall not:

- use User Images for advertising, marketing or public display;
- disclose User Images to Vendors, other Users or any person other than a processor engaged under Clause 5;
- sell, trade or transfer User Images; or
- use User Images to train, fine-tune or improve any artificial intelligence or machine-learning model, unless You give separate, distinct and express opt-in consent for that purpose, which You may withdraw at any time.

3.6 Processing. Processing is automated and consists of image segmentation, body-shape estimation and synthesis of the rendering. No human review is undertaken save where necessary to investigate a complaint, a suspected contravention of the Terms of Use, or a direction of an authority.

3.7 Retention. User Images are retained only so long as necessary for the purpose in Clause 3.3 and in any event not beyond 24 hours from upload, or until withdrawal of consent, whichever is earlier, whereafter they are erased, save where retention is required by Applicable Law or for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

3.8 Withdrawal of consent. You may withdraw consent at any time through Platform settings or by writing to the Grievance Officer. On withdrawal We shall cease further processing and cause erasure in accordance with Clause 3.7. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it. The consequence of withdrawal is that the AI Virtual Try-On will cease to be available to You.

3.9 You retain all rights in Your User Image. No proprietary right therein is transferred to Us.
