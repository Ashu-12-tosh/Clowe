# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 16 October 2026*

This policy explains what personal data Clowe collects, why, who sees it and how long we keep it. **{{legal.name}}**, which owns and runs cloweshop.com, is responsible for it.

## What we collect

- **Your account** — phone number, name, email if you add one, and the addresses you save.
- **Your orders** — what you bought, amounts, delivery address, and the payment method and status. Card and UPI details are entered with our payment gateway; we never see or store card numbers.
- **Try-on photos** — the photo you upload to use AI Try-On, and the try-on images made from it.
- **Messages to us** — support chats, complaints, and photos you add to a return.
- **Searches** — the words typed into search, without your name or account, to improve results.
- **Sellers** — business details, GSTIN, PAN, bank account, pickup and return addresses, and packing videos.

## How we use it

- To run your account, take payment, deliver your order, and handle cancellations, returns and refunds.
- To send OTPs and order updates by SMS or WhatsApp.
- To make try-on images when you ask for them.
- To prevent fraud and keep the site secure.
- To meet tax and legal duties (for example, GST invoices and records).

## Who we share it with

Only what each one needs, and only to do the job:

- **The seller of your order** — your name, delivery address and phone, to fulfil it.
- **Our courier partner (Delhivery)** — name, address and phone, to deliver, collect returns and return undelivered parcels.
- **Our payment gateway** — to take payments and make refunds.
- **Our SMS / WhatsApp provider** — to send OTPs and order updates.
- **Our AI try-on provider** — your try-on photo and the product image, only to make your try-on image.
- **Our identity-check provider** (sellers only) — PAN, GSTIN and bank account details, to verify them.
- **Government authorities** — when the law requires it.

We never sell your personal data.

## How long we keep it

- **Try-on photos** are deleted {{tryonPhotoDays}} after you upload them — unless you save one as your try-on photo to reuse. A saved photo stays until you delete it (Profile → Try-on photo).
- **Searches** are kept for 90 days.
- **Packing videos** (filmed by sellers) are kept until {{packingVideoDays}} after delivery, and for as long as a return on that order is open.
- **Orders, invoices and payment records** are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.
- **Your account** is kept until you ask us to delete it.

## Your choices

- Change your details and notification preferences in **Account Settings**.
- Delete your saved try-on photo at any time.
- To see, correct or delete your data, or to close your account, write to {{customerSupportEmail}}.
