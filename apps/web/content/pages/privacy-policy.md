# Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what Clowe collects and why.

## What we collect

- **Account data** — phone number, name and addresses you save.
- **Order data** — items purchased, payments and delivery details.
- **Usage data** — pages viewed, searches and device information, to improve the experience.
- **Try-On photos** — used only to generate your try-on preview; you can delete your saved photo anytime.

## What we never do

- We never sell your personal data.
- We never store full card numbers — payments are handled by certified gateways.

## Your controls

Manage notification preferences and personal details from **Account Settings**, or contact support to delete your account.
