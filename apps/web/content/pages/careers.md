# Careers at Clowe

We're a small team building the future of fashion commerce in India — AI try-on, multi-vendor logistics, and delightful shopping experiences.

## Open roles

We're always happy to hear from great people in:

- **Engineering** — full-stack (TypeScript/Node/React), ML/computer vision
- **Design** — product design, fashion styling for AI imagery
- **Operations** — seller onboarding, logistics partnerships
- **Marketing** — growth, content, social

## How to apply

Email your resume and a short note to **careers@clowe.example** with the role in the subject line. We reply to every application.

## Life at Clowe

Remote-friendly, small ego-free team, direct ownership of what you ship. If a feature goes live, your name is on it.
