# Advertise Your Products

Put your products in front of more shoppers with **Clowe Ads** — admin-reviewed promoted placements that are always clearly labeled "Sponsored".

## Placements

- **Home page banner** — a premium slot on the Clowe home page
- **Sponsored in category** — top placement in your product's category listing

## How it works

1. Open your **Seller Dashboard → Advertise**
2. Pick a live product, a placement, and a duration (7 / 15 / 30 days)
3. See the price up-front and submit — our team reviews every ad
4. Once approved, your ad goes live instantly and auto-expires at the end of its run

Billing is simple for now: the ad amount is **adjusted from your seller payouts** — no online payment needed.

## Track results

Your dashboard shows live **views and clicks** for every ad, so you know exactly what you got.
