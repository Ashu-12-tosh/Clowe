# About Clowe

Clowe is a multi-vendor online marketplace - electronics, mobiles, fashion, home & kitchen, beauty, books, groceries and more - built around one idea: **shopping should feel as sure as seeing it in person**.

Our AI Try-On lets you upload a single photo and instantly preview t-shirts, dresses, jackets and more — on you, not on a model. Combined with personalized recommendations and a curated seller network across every department, Clowe makes online shopping feel like being in the store.

## What we stand for

- **Fit first** — fewer returns, happier wardrobes
- **Fair marketplace** — every seller, from boutique to brand, gets the same tools
- **Privacy by default** — your try-on photo is yours; delete it anytime from your profile

## The journey

Clowe started in 2026 as a simple experiment: could AI make "how will this look on me?" a solved problem? Today we host thousands of products across electronics, fashion, home, beauty, books and more, with virtual try-on on our fashion range.
