# FAQ

*Last updated: 16 October 2026*

## Orders

- **How do I track my order?** Use [Track Order](/track) with your order number and phone, or open it in **My Orders**.
- **Can I cancel?** Yes, until any item in the order has shipped. Cancelling cancels the whole order — see [Cancellation](/pages/cancellation).
- **Is there a delivery charge?** No. Delivery is free on every order, with no minimum order value.
- **How long does delivery take?** Usually 4–6 business days for standard delivery. Checkout shows the estimated date for your pincode.

## Returns and refunds

- **What is the return window?** {{returnWindow}} from delivery for most items. Some categories, such as grocery, have a shorter window — each product page shows it. See the [Return Policy](/pages/return-policy).
- **Do I pay for the return pickup?** No. The pickup is free for you.
- **When do I get my refund?** For a cancelled order, straight away. For a return, once the item reaches the seller and is checked. Banks usually show it within 5–7 business days.

## Payments

- **How can I pay?** UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking, or Cash on Delivery for orders up to {{codMax}}.
- **Is GST included?** Yes. Prices include GST ({{gst}} on all products).
- **Is it safe?** Payments are processed by PCI-DSS compliant gateways. Clowe never stores card numbers.

## AI Try-On

- **How does it work?** Upload one clear photo of yourself and try eligible products on from their product page.
- **What happens to my photo?** It is used only to make your try-on images, and is deleted {{tryonPhotoDays}} after upload unless you save it for next time. See the [Privacy Policy](/pages/privacy-policy).
