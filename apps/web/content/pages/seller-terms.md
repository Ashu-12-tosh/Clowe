# Seller Terms

*Last updated: 16 October 2026*

These terms are an agreement between you, a seller on Clowe, and **{{legal.name}}**, which runs the cloweshop.com marketplace. By registering as a seller you agree to them, as well as to the [Terms & Conditions](/pages/terms-conditions).

## Clowe is a marketplace

- You sell your products to buyers directly; Clowe runs the site, collects payment from buyers on your behalf, arranges delivery and handles buyer complaints.
- {{legal.name}} also sells on Clowe through its own **Clowe Official** store, under the same rules as every other seller.

## Getting approved

- Register with your business details. A **GSTIN is required** before your store can be approved — every tax invoice to a buyer is issued under it.
- Your GSTIN must carry your PAN (characters 3–12), and both are checked before approval.
- You can be paid only once your **PAN is verified**, along with your bank account.

## Listing and pricing

- You set your price before GST. Buyers pay your price plus GST ({{gst}} on all products), and the GST reaches you with your earnings for you to file.
- Delivery is free for buyers; you pay the courier's actual charges (see below).
- Your listings must be accurate: what you show is what you ship.

## Packing and dispatch

- **Packing video:** record a short video of every order going into its box before you mark it packed or ship it — the order cannot move on without one. Only you and Clowe's team can see it, never the buyer. It is used to settle disputes, and is kept until {{packingVideoDays}} after delivery, and for as long as a return is open.
- **Dispatch within {{dispatchSla}}** of the order being placed, with the Clowe shipping label.
- **Late dispatch:** an order not dispatched within {{penaltyAfter}} of being placed is charged {{penaltyAmount}}, once per order.
- **Seller cancellation:** if you cancel an order, you are charged {{sellerCancelPenalty}}, and the buyer is refunded in full.

## Returns

- Buyers can return items within {{returnWindow}} of delivery (shorter for some categories, such as grocery), unless your store sets a more generous window.
- Approve or decline every return request within 48 hours; a decline needs a reason the buyer can see. Clowe's team can override a decline.
- Approved returns are picked up from the buyer by Delhivery and delivered to the **return address in your Store Settings**, or to your pickup address if you have not set one. The return pickup charge is yours.
- When the item reaches you, mark it received. If it is fine, the buyer is refunded automatically. If it is damaged, mark it damaged — Clowe's team reviews the case, using your packing video.

## Fees and charges

Your charges are listed on the [Seller Fees](/pages/seller-fees) page, and the exact amounts on every order are on your Payouts page.

## Getting paid

- You earn on **delivery**, not on order.
- Earnings are held for **{{payoutHold}}** after delivery, to cover returns, then become payable. The minimum payout is {{payoutMin}}.
- **Cash on Delivery** money becomes payable only after the courier has paid it to Clowe and it is matched to your parcel.
- Every payout is net of Clowe's fees, the courier charges, any penalties, and the tax deducted by law (see below).

## Tax deducted by law

- **TDS under section 194-O** of the Income Tax Act: {{tds}} of the sale value, deducted against your PAN.
- **TCS under section 52** of the CGST Act: {{tcs}} of the taxable value, collected and reported against your GSTIN.
- Both are deposited with the government and shown in your reports for filing.

## Ending

Clowe can suspend or close a store that breaks these terms — for example, for fake or unsafe products, misleading listings, or repeated late dispatch or cancellations.
