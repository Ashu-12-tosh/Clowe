# Recalls and Product Safety Alerts

Your safety comes first. This page lists product recalls and safety notices affecting items sold on Clowe.

## Current alerts

There are **no active recalls** at this time.

## How recalls work

1. If a safety issue is identified, the affected listings are removed immediately
2. Every buyer of the affected item is notified by SMS/WhatsApp and in-app notification
3. Returns and full refunds are arranged automatically — no return-window limits apply to recalled items

## Report a safety concern

If a product you bought on Clowe seems unsafe, contact us via the Support chat or raise a complaint from your account. Include photos where possible.
