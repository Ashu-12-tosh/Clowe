# Clowe Science

The technology behind "see it on yourself".

## AI Try-On

Our try-on pipeline combines pose estimation, garment segmentation and generative imaging to place a product on your photo realistically — preserving your pose, body shape and lighting.

- **One photo, many outfits** — upload once, try the whole catalogue
- **Provider-agnostic** — our engine can swap between in-house and partner models
- **Cost-aware** — every generation is metered and quality-checked

## Recommendations

Clowe learns from what you browse, wishlist and buy to rank products you're more likely to love — and lets you switch personalization off in your profile at any time.

## Responsible AI

Try-on photos are stored securely, used only for your try-ons, and deletable anytime. We never use your photos to train models without explicit consent.
